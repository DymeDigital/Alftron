About Alftron

Delivering premium fire and security protection with clarity and confidence.

Alftron brings specialised experience in fire alarm systems, suppression solutions, and fully integrated security systems for businesses and homes. We make safety easier with clear guidance, trusted installation, and ongoing support.

  • Design and system planning
  • Professional installation and commissioning
  • Inspection, testing, and certification
  • Maintenance and monitoring support

For more than 25 years, Alftron has helped businesses and homeowners across South Africa protect what matters most. What began as a specialised fire protection company has grown into a full-service fire and security partner, trusted on everything from single-site installations to complex industrial and commercial projects.

Our approach is built on doing the job properly the first time. Every system we design is tailored to the specific risks of your site, installed by certified technicians, and backed by the inspection, testing and maintenance needed to keep it compliant and dependable for years. We work to SANS standards and insurance requirements, so you can be confident your protection will perform when it counts.
