About Us

Delivering premium fire and security protection with clarity and confidence.

Alftron Fire and Security is a leading provider of integrated fire protection and security solutions, serving clients across South Africa and the African continent. We are committed to protecting lives, safeguarding assets, and delivering innovative, compliant solutions that meet the highest industry standards.

  • Design and system planning
  • Professional installation and commissioning
  • Inspection, testing, and certification
  • Maintenance and monitoring support

Recognised for our technical expertise, exceptional service, and competitive pricing, we provide cost-effective solutions without compromising on quality, safety, or reliability. Every project is executed with precision, professionalism, and an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our team comprises SAQCC-certified professionals, experienced ASIB-approved engineers, and highly skilled technicians with extensive industry expertise. We work in accordance with SABS and SANS standards, ensuring our solutions meet recognised South African quality and safety requirements. As a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor, we are committed to delivering excellence, building lasting partnerships, and providing trusted fire protection and security solutions throughout Africa.
